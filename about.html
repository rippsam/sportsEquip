About

This started as a completely normal sports equipment store. Clean layout, motivational headlines, products that promised to take your performance to the next level—Move without limits. And to be fair, that’s still technically true. Somewhere along the way, this site became more about what happens when you mix athletics with development. Now every product doubles as a gentle reminder of what bothers you about software in meme form.

We’re still selling the gear (if you can make an order), but at this point it’s just as much a playground for our QA minds as a retail site. Whether you’re here to shop, explore, accidentally discover a bug or just enjoy some memes, welcome.
